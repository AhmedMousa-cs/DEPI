# Golden Project B — GreenCart grocery delivery

GreenCart is a small grocery store that wants to sell online and deliver to customers
inside the city. The store already has a paper-based stock list and a cash register.

Goals:
- Let customers order groceries online and choose a delivery slot.
- Let the store manager keep the product list and stock levels up to date.
- Give customers clear order status updates from checkout to delivery.
- Keep customer and payment data safe.

Out of scope for the first release: loyalty points, supplier portals, and multi-branch support.
