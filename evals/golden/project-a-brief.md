# Project A: Clinic Appointment Booking System

## Goal
Build a web application that allows patients to book clinic appointments online and enables receptionists and doctors to manage appointments digitally instead of relying on paper records and phone calls.

## Users
- Patient: Search for doctors or specialties, book appointments, cancel bookings, and reschedule appointments.
- Receptionist: View the daily schedule, book appointments for patients over the phone, and confirm patient attendance.
- Doctor: Define available appointment slots and view their schedule.
- Clinic Administrator: Manage doctors, specialties, and reports.

## Approximate Requirements
- Login for patients and staff.
- Browse doctors by specialty and view available appointment slots.
- Book, cancel, and reschedule appointments.
- Appointment reminders through in-app notifications.
- Dashboards for receptionists and doctors showing the daily schedule.
- Monthly report showing the number of bookings.

## Constraints
- The team consists of four people.
- Patient data is sensitive, so access permissions must be clearly defined.
- The application must work well on mobile devices.

## Open Questions
- Will online payments be supported?
- Will the system support multiple clinic branches?
