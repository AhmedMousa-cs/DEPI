# Golden Project C - FixMate home services

FixMate is a home-repair marketplace. Customers book a vetted technician for plumbing,
electrical or appliance work, a dispatcher assigns the nearest available person, and the
technician updates the job from a phone on site.

The first release is one city and one language. The partner pilot starts on 1 December 2026,
so the release date is fixed.

Goals:
- Customers can register, save an address and book a service with a time slot.
- A dispatcher can assign the nearest available technician to every job.
- Technicians can accept a job and move it through on the way, working and done.
- Customers pay by card at booking and can rate the technician afterwards.
- The admin can see daily jobs, revenue and technician load.
- Roles are enforced for customers, technicians, dispatchers and admins.

Out of scope for the first release: recurring maintenance contracts, spare-parts
inventory, and multi-city coverage.
