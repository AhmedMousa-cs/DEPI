PM: GreenCart wants a simple online store for groceries with delivery inside the city.

Store manager: I need to add new products and change the stock every morning. If an item
is out of stock I want it hidden from customers right away.

Customer: I want to search for what I need and put it in a basket. At checkout I want to
pick a delivery slot. I can pay by card online or cash when the order arrives.

PM: Customers must see the order status: accepted, packed, out for delivery and delivered.

Security lead: Payment data must not be stored on our servers, and only the manager can
change products or see the daily numbers.

Decision: the first release is single city and single store; multi-branch comes later.
